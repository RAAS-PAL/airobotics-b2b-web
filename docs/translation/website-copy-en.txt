HOW TO USE THIS FILE
---------------------
Every string has a [KEY] identifying where it lives on the site, an
EN: line with the English source, and a blank TH: line underneath.
Type the Thai translation directly on the TH: line and send the file
back — do not change the [KEY] lines, they are used to put each
translation back in the right place on the site.

Keep the same tone as the English where you can: direct, plain-spoken,
aimed at a facility manager or operations director — not a marketing
brochure voice. Sentences are often short on purpose.

DO NOT TRANSLATE — leave these exactly as-is wherever they appear
-------------------------------------------------------------------

  Robot model names:     L3, L4, L50, SP50, S5
  Technology/part names: NVIDIA, LiDAR, TOPS, TeamClean, WS3, WT3, CWS
  Units of measurement:  mm, cm, m, kg, L, gal, in, ft, h, min, kPa,
                          m²/h, TOPS, dB, kg, Ah, W
  Numbers and specs themselves (700, 21,674, 96-beam, etc.)
  URLs, email addresses and anything in braces (airoboticsth.com, {email}, {model})

Numerals: use whichever numeral style your team prefers for Thai
(Thai numerals ๐-๙ or Arabic 0-9 as commonly used in Thai business
writing) — just be consistent throughout.

[HOME.faq.tops_q]
EN: What does "100 TOPS AI compute" mean?
TH: 

[HOME.faq.tops_a]
EN: TOPS stands for trillion operations per second — a measure of how much AI processing the robot's onboard chip can do. On a cleaning machine that capacity goes on reading the LiDAR and cameras many times a second: spotting obstacles, planning a route, and recognising what is on the floor. More TOPS means the machine can run heavier perception models and react faster in a busy space. The L3 and S5 carry 100 TOPS of NVIDIA compute; the L4 carries 32 TOPS.
TH: 

[HOME.faq.which_q]
EN: Which of the five machines do we need?
TH: 

[HOME.faq.which_a]
EN: It depends on the floor rather than the building. The L3 passes through 700 mm gaps, for tight retail, hospitals and schools. The L4 clears a standard doorway at 810 mm and scrubs to within 3 cm of the wall. The L50 is built for warehouse-scale open floor. The S5 sweeps dry debris in industrial settings. The SP50 patrols for scattered mess instead of scrubbing everything. Tell us the size, floor type and operating hours and we will recommend one.

================================================================
Each robot (L3, L4, L50, SP50, S5) has the same shape: a kind, tagline,
one-line pitch, three highlight bullets, a "best for" label, a
"pick this if" paragraph, an intro paragraph, four headline stats,
five feature blocks (each with an eyebrow label, title, body text,
and often three bullet points), spec table group titles and spec
labels, and a list of environments it's used in.

Spec VALUES (numbers like "700 mm" or "100 TOPS") do not need
translation — only the LABELS next to them do (e.g. "Passage width",
"Max productivity").
